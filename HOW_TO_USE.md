# How Contractors Use This App

## Overview
This is a comprehensive contractor management app designed for trade professionals (landscaping, roofing, plumbing, HVAC, etc.) to manage their entire business from lead to payment.

## Key Workflows

### 1. **Managing Clients**
- **Add New Clients**: From estimates or clients tab, create new client profiles with contact info, property addresses, and special notes
- **Track Client History**: View all jobs, estimates, payments, and interactions for each client
- **Set Client Budgets**: Admin can set and track client budgets to ensure estimates stay within their financial constraints
- **Reliability Scoring**: System tracks payment history, communication quality, and referrals

### 2. **Creating Estimates**
**From Estimates Tab → New Estimate**
- Select existing client or create new one
- Add line items with quantities, rates, and units
- System auto-calculates subtotal, tax, and total
- Add notes and terms
- Save as draft or send to client via email
- Track estimate status: Draft → Sent → Approved/Declined

**Budget Awareness**: If client has a budget set, the estimate screen shows:
- Available budget
- Current estimate total
- Remaining budget
- Warnings if estimate exceeds budget

### 3. **Managing Jobs**
**From Jobs Tab → Job Detail**

**Before Job:**
- Review job details, client info, and arrival instructions
- Check assigned crew members
- View linked estimate
- Plan with checklist

**During Job:**
- Mark crew arrival time
- Start work timer
- Complete checklist items (fully customizable - add, edit, delete)
- Take before/during/after photos organized by type
- Track materials used
- Upload receipts for expenses

**After Job:**
- Complete final checklist items
- Upload after photos
- Add job notes
- Mark job as complete
- Track actual cost vs budget

**Edit Capabilities:**
- Edit job details (service type, address, costs, notes)
- Customize checklist (add new items, edit existing, delete unnecessary ones)
- All changes persist immediately

### 4. **Creating Contracts**
**From Contracts → New Contract**
- Choose from 16+ contract types (MSA, Project Contract, Work Order, Time & Materials, Fixed Price, etc.)
- Select client and enter project details
- Choose services from your trade-specific list
- Set payment milestones with percentages
- Add warranty terms and additional notes
- **Save as Draft**: Drafts automatically appear in the Contracts section
- **Send for E-Signature**: Email contract to client for digital signing

**Saved Drafts**: All saved contract drafts show up immediately in the contracts list with "Draft" status, allowing you to:
- Continue editing later
- Review before sending
- Track which contracts are still in draft vs sent/signed

### 5. **Financial Tracking** (Admin Only)
- Lifetime value per client
- Outstanding balances
- Job profitability (budgeted vs actual cost)
- Receipt tracking and categorization
- Payment history

### 6. **Crew Features**
Crew members have a simplified view:
- See assigned jobs
- Access arrival instructions
- Complete checklists
- Upload photos
- Track time
- Cannot see financials or client details

### 7. **Communication**
From any job or client detail:
- Call client directly
- Email client
- Navigate to property
- View job-linked estimates

## Mobile-First Design
- Works on phones and tablets
- Camera integration for job photos
- GPS navigation to job sites
- Offline capability with AsyncStorage
- Cross-platform (iOS, Android, Web)

## Admin vs Crew Access
**Admin Users:**
- Full access to financials
- Create/edit estimates and contracts
- Manage clients and crew
- View all analytics

**Crew Users:**
- View assigned jobs
- Complete checklists
- Upload photos
- Track time
- Limited to operational tasks

## Key Benefits
1. **Everything in One Place**: Clients, estimates, jobs, contracts, and payments
2. **Real-Time Updates**: Changes sync across devices immediately
3. **Professional Presentation**: Clean, modern interface impresses clients
4. **Accountability**: Photo documentation, time tracking, and checklists
5. **Financial Control**: Budget tracking, cost monitoring, and profitability analysis
6. **Flexible Contracts**: 16+ contract types for any business model
7. **Smart Features**: Budget warnings, reliability scores, progress tracking

## Typical Contractor Day

**Morning:**
1. Check daily schedule
2. Review job details and client notes
3. Navigate to first job

**On Site:**
1. Mark arrival
2. Take before photos
3. Complete checklist as you work
4. Upload receipts for materials
5. Take during/after photos
6. Mark job complete

**Evening:**
1. Review completed jobs
2. Create estimates for new leads
3. Send contracts for upcoming projects
4. Track financials and profitability

## Getting Started
1. **Set Up Company Profile**: Enter business name, trade type, and contact info
2. **Add Clients**: Import or manually add client contact information
3. **Create Templates**: Set up common estimate line items and contract templates
4. **Invite Crew**: Generate crew codes for team members to join
5. **Start Managing Jobs**: Create estimates, convert to jobs, and track progress

This app streamlines the entire contractor workflow from initial client contact through final payment collection.
